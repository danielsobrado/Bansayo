## Alternative without an LED rig

A 240 fps phone films both a clap (or a light switched by hand) and a monitor showing the
engine's camera preview (`GET /api/v1/cameras/{id}/preview.jpg` in a browser, refreshing) next to
a millisecond clock. Count phone frames from the event to its first appearance in the preview;
subtract the preview path (one preview period, 100 ms maximum, and the browser's display delay)
measured by filming the clock alone. This bounds exposure → arrival from above with ± 4 ms
resolution plus the preview period; record it as an upper bound, not as L-01.
