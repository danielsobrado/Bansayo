## Method

Guided sessions, ≥ 30 roundhouse kicks per side per person, inputs recorded. Each take promoted
with `bansayo-engine promote`, annotated with `scripts/annotate_reps.py`, evaluated with
`bansayo-engine regress <golden>/manifest.yaml` (matching: IoU ≥ 0.5).
